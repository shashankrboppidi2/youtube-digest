## Lior Susan on why gross margin is a vanity metric

**Sourcery with Molly O'Shea** · 1:54 · Oct 03 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3bNqZp8AwWo)

**TL;DR:** Lior Susan argues that gross margin is a misleading metric for public companies, suggesting that free cash flow is more important. He explains that while a high gross margin might indicate a strong business, it doesn't guarantee profitability or market success.

**Worth watching in full?** 5/5 — The summary cannot replace the detailed insights and examples provided in the video.

**Bookmarks:**
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/3bNqZp8AwWo?t=0) Introduction and definition of gross margin

**Key takeaways:**

- The key to a successful company lies in understanding the market size, differentiation, and having the right team to solve tough problems.
