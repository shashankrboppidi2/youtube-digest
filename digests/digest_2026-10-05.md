He uses the example of cryptocurrency, where many investors follow others who have made money in the space, rather than considering the long-term sustainability of the trend. This behavior is exacerbated by the availability of information and the tendency to extrapolate short-term trends into the future without considering their feasibility.
- [4:01](https://youtu.be/drg9CuuIY6A?t=241) - Edmunds mentions a study by John Coates, a trader turned neurobiologist, which showed that traders' hormones affect their behavior, leading to devastating decisions. - [5:01](https://youtu.be/drg9CuuIY6A?t=301) - Edmunds discusses how the market feels more negatively about losses than gains, a phenomenon known as loss aversion.
