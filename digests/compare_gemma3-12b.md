# Finding The 1% of Stocks That Matter | Henry Ellenbogen Interview

**Invest Like The Best** · 1:54:44 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plx6Foxoam4)

**TL;DR:** Henry Ellenbogen, founder of Durable Capital, discusses his investment philosophy, which centers on identifying the "1%" of companies – roughly 40 out of 4,000 – that compound wealth significantly over time. He emphasizes the importance of balance, resilience, understanding people and change, and leveraging AI to improve existing businesses, often through partnerships with exceptional leaders.

**Worth watching in full?** 3/5 — While this summary captures the core ideas, the interview's value lies in Ellenbogen's detailed explanations, anecdotes, and the nuanced way he connects his philosophy to specific companies and experiences.

**Key takeaways:**
- Only roughly 1% of stocks generate exceptional returns (6x) over 10 years, typically starting as small-cap companies.
- Successful companies require a balance of growth, innovation, and profitability, not just one area.
- "Act Two" entrepreneurs, leveraging prior experience, often build successful companies.
- AI presents significant opportunities for established companies to improve efficiency and gain a cost advantage.
- Durable Capital focuses on long-term partnerships with portfolio companies, sharing insights and data.
- Continuous learning and adaptation are crucial, especially in the face of disruptive technologies like AI.
