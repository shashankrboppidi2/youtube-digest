**Key takeaways:**
- Henry Ellenbogen focuses on finding the 1% of stocks that matter by investing in small companies with strong operating cultures and resilient leadership.
- Henry's team looks for common elements among successful companies, such as having a diverse portfolio across different sectors.
- Henry's team focuses on understanding the impact of AI on existing companies and the potential for a second wave of Kaizen in robotics.
- Henry's team looks for companies that can use technology to improve their physical business models.
- He emphasizes the importance of understanding the trajectory of a company's competitive advantage and its potential to benefit from the curve shift.
- He uses KPI reviews to ensure alignment with expectations and learn from past decisions.
- He believes the path to building a compounder or generational company through the public markets is proven, and understanding it correctly can be very clear.
